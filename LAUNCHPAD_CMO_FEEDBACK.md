# Launchpad Platform Feedback: CMO Perspective
**Role:** Chief Marketing Officer, Premium Pet Care Brand  
**Date:** October 30, 2025  
**Evaluation Period:** Comprehensive platform usage for marketing plan development

---

## Executive Summary

As a CMO tasked with developing a comprehensive marketing plan, I utilized the full breadth of Sovrn Launchpad's capabilities. This feedback represents real-world usage across all major features: Audience Insights, U.S. Market Intelligence, Campaign Content Generator, and Deal Library. Below is my assessment of what provided the most and least value, followed by actionable product improvement recommendations.

---

## Feature Value Assessment

### 🏆 MOST VALUABLE FEATURES

#### 1. **Commerce Audience Insights Reports** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
**Value Rating: 10/10**

**What Worked Exceptionally Well:**
- **Psychographic Depth:** The persona generation ("Family-Focused Urban Pet Parent") went beyond demographics to paint a vivid picture of daily life, motivations, and behaviors
- **Executive Summary Quality:** The 300-word summary was immediately usable in presentations and briefs—no editing required
- **Data Richness:** Combining census data, behavioral patterns, and geographic concentration in one report saved hours of manual research
- **Cross-Purchase Insights:** The behavioral overlap data (17% with Home & Garden, 17% with Furniture) immediately sparked partnership ideas I wouldn't have considered

**Business Impact:**
> This single feature replaced what would have been $15K+ in market research spending. The insights were specific enough to inform creative briefing, media planning, and partnership strategy simultaneously.

**Why It's Valuable:**
- **Time Savings:** Generated in 10-15 seconds vs. 2-3 weeks for traditional research
- **Actionability:** Every data point translated directly into a strategic decision
- **Credibility:** Census-backed data gave confidence when presenting to executives

---

#### 2. **U.S. Market Insights (Geographic Intelligence)** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
**Value Rating: 9/10**

**What Worked Exceptionally Well:**
- **Metric Flexibility:** Being able to sort by 18 different metrics (income, education, age, etc.) allowed me to explore markets from multiple angles
- **Multi-Level Geography:** The ability to analyze by CBSA, then drill down to ZIP codes was perfect for media planning at different scales
- **Opportunity Scoring:** The built-in "opportunity score" helped prioritize markets beyond just raw size or wealth
- **National Benchmarking:** Seeing how each market compared to national averages made strengths/weaknesses immediately obvious

**Business Impact:**
> Identified the San Francisco Bay Area as a $4.8M population, $141K median income market in 30 seconds. This became the anchor of my market strategy.

**Minor Limitations:**
- Would benefit from filtering by multiple metrics simultaneously (e.g., "high income AND high pet ownership")
- Missing competitive saturation data (are these markets already crowded with pet brands?)

---

#### 3. **Campaign Content Generator (AI Briefs)** ⭐⭐⭐⭐
**Value Rating: 8/10**

**What Worked Exceptionally Well:**
- **Market-Specific Messaging:** Headlines like "Elevate Your Bay Area Lifestyle" captured regional pride and demographic sophistication
- **Prioritized Value Props:** The 3-tier structure (Luxury, Cultural Resonance, Investment) gave clear hierarchy for messaging
- **A/B Test Ready:** Generated 5 headline variants immediately usable for testing
- **Creative Direction:** The persona and headlines provided clear creative briefing for agency partners

**Where It Fell Short:**
- **Generic at Times:** Some output felt formulaic ("Discover Unmatched Quality")
- **Lack of Brand Voice:** No ability to input brand guidelines or tone preferences
- **No Product Integration:** Briefs were market-focused but didn't reference actual product features or benefits

**Business Impact:**
> Saved 3-4 days of creative briefing work, but still required human refinement for brand voice.

---

#### 4. **Deal Library (Media Execution)** ⭐⭐⭐⭐
**Value Rating: 7/10**

**What Worked Exceptionally Well:**
- **Precise Targeting:** Found 4 pet-specific deals across CTV, Multi-Format, and Mobile App Video
- **Format Variety:** Having options across premium (CTV) and performance (Mobile App) channels allowed budget flexibility
- **Commerce Audience Targeting:** All deals leveraged verified purchase intent data, reducing waste
- **Deal IDs Ready:** Could immediately share deal IDs with media team for activation

**Where It Fell Short:**
- **Limited Inventory:** Only 4 deals for "pet" search—would have liked more variety
- **No CPM Visibility:** Had to request pricing separately; would prefer transparency upfront
- **Missing Deal Details:** No information on creative specs, targeting parameters, or historical performance
- **No Competitive Context:** Can't see if competitors are already using these deals

**Business Impact:**
> Provided clear media plan foundation, but required significant follow-up with sales team for execution details.

---

### 🤔 LEAST VALUABLE FEATURES

#### 1. **Audiences Tool (Taxonomy Search)** ⭐⭐
**Value Rating: 3/10**

**Why It Underperformed:**
- **Search Didn't Work Well:** Natural language query for "premium pet food brand targeting affluent pet owners" returned ZERO results despite 1,342 segments being loaded
- **No Browsing Experience:** Couldn't explore the taxonomy hierarchy—had to know exact segment names
- **Redundant with Audience Insights:** The Audience Insights Report tool provided deeper data without needing to search first
- **Unclear Value Prop:** Not sure when I'd use taxonomy search vs. just going straight to insights reports

**What Would Make It Better:**
- Browse-able category tree (expand "Pets > Dog Supplies > Premium Food")
- Keyword matching that works (even simple "pet" should return pet-related segments)
- Show segment scale/CPM in search results to aid selection
- Integration with Deal Library (show available deals next to each segment)

**Business Impact:**
> Abandoned this tool after 2 failed searches. Used Audience Insights Report directly instead.

---

#### 2. **Strategy Cards Section** ⭐⭐⭐
**Value Rating: 5/10**

**Why It Underperformed:**
- **Didn't Discover It:** The feature wasn't prominent enough in navigation; only learned about it from documentation
- **Unclear Use Case:** Not sure how Strategy Cards differ from Audience Insights Reports or Campaign Briefs
- **No Testing Performed:** Didn't use this feature during my marketing plan development

**What Would Make It Better:**
- Clearer distinction from other features
- Suggested starting point ("New to Launchpad? Start with Strategy Cards")
- Integration into workflow (e.g., "Save this Audience Insight as a Strategy Card")

## Overall Platform Assessment

### Strengths 💪

1. **AI-Powered Speed:** Generated a complete marketing plan in under 30 minutes that would have taken weeks manually
2. **Data Integration:** Seamlessly combining census data, commerce behavior, and geographic insights is incredibly powerful
3. **End-to-End Workflow:** From audience research → market selection → campaign briefs → media deals in one platform
4. **Output Quality:** The generated content (personas, headlines, insights) was professional and immediately usable
5. **Scale:** Access to 199 audience segments, 33K+ ZIP codes, 18 demographic metrics, and real deal inventory

### Weaknesses 😐

1. **Search Functionality:** Natural language search underperformed expectations
2. **Feature Discoverability:** Some tools were hard to find or understand when to use
3. **Limited Customization:** Can't input brand voice, competitive context, or constraints
4. **No Collaboration Tools:** Can't share plans, get feedback, or assign tasks to team members
5. **Missing Integrations:** No export to campaign management tools, CRMs, or presentation software

## Product Improvement Recommendations

### 🚀 HIGH PRIORITY (Implement First)

#### 1. **Fix Audiences Tool Search**
**Problem:** Returns zero results for natural language queries  
**Solution:**
- Implement fuzzy matching and keyword extraction
- Add browse-by-category interface
- Show "suggested segments" based on query intent
- Display segment stats (CPM, scale, active) in search results

**Expected Impact:** 10x increase in Audiences Tool usage

---

#### 2. **Add CPM & Deal Details to Deal Library**
**Problem:** Can't evaluate deal viability without external pricing info  
**Solution:**
- Display CPM range directly in search results
- Add "Deal Details" modal with:
  - Historical performance benchmarks
  - Creative specifications
  - Targeting parameters (GEO, device, daypart)
  - Competitive usage indicators
- Include "Request Quote" button for custom pricing

**Expected Impact:** Reduce sales team follow-up by 60%

---

#### 3. **Introduce "Marketing Plan Builder" Workflow**
**Problem:** Users must manually stitch together insights from multiple tools  
**Solution:**
Create a guided workflow:
```
Step 1: Select Your Product Category
  → Auto-suggests relevant audience segments

Step 2: Choose Your Audiences
  → Auto-generates insights reports for selected segments

Step 3: Identify Priority Markets
  → Auto-loads U.S. Market Insights for audience concentrations

Step 4: Generate Campaign Briefs
  → Auto-creates market-specific messaging for each market

Step 5: Build Media Plan
  → Auto-populates Deal Library with relevant deals

Step 6: Export Complete Plan
  → PDF, PowerPoint, or Google Docs with all insights
```

**Expected Impact:** 50% reduction in time-to-plan completion

---

#### 4. **Add Brand Customization Settings**
**Problem:** AI-generated content lacks brand voice and product context  
**Solution:**
- User profile with brand settings:
  - Brand voice (Professional / Casual / Playful / Technical)
  - Key product features (upload product sheets)
  - Competitive positioning (who you compete with)
  - Messaging guardrails (avoid certain words/concepts)
- Apply settings to all AI-generated content

**Expected Impact:** 80% reduction in content editing time

---

### 🎯 MEDIUM PRIORITY (Implement Second)

#### 5. **Competitive Intelligence Layer**
**Problem:** No visibility into what competitors are doing  
**Solution:**
- "Market Saturation Score" for each audience segment
- "Competitor Activity" indicator on deals (who else is buying)

**Expected Impact:** Strategic advantage in market selection

---

#### 6. **Collaboration & Workflow Features**
**Problem:** Can't share work or collaborate with team  
**Solution:**

#### 7. **Budget Optimization Engine**
**Problem:** Still manually calculating budget allocations

Add "Budget Optimizer" tool:

- "What-if" modeling (e.g., "What if I shift $50K from CTV to Mobile?")

**Expected Impact:** 25% improvement in media efficiency

- Automated optimization recommendations

**Expected Impact:** Closed-loop marketing intelligence

## Usability & Experience Feedback

### What Worked Well ✅

1. **Fast Load Times:** Every API call returned results in <15 seconds
2. **Clean UI:** Sovrn brand colors and typography created professional aesthetic
3. **Intuitive Layout:** Two-panel layouts (search + results) were easy to navigate
4. **Copy Functionality:** One-click copy buttons saved time when building the plan
5. **No Learning Curve:** Could start using immediately without training

### What Needs Improvement ⚠️

1. **No Onboarding:** Would benefit from interactive tutorial or sample workflow
2. **Feature Overwhelm:** 5+ major tools with unclear hierarchy—where do I start?
3. **No Help Documentation:** Had to rely on external .md files to understand features
4. **Mobile Experience:** Didn't test, but suspect complex dashboards won't translate well
5. **Export Options:** Copy-paste was tedious; need "Export to PDF" or "Export to PowerPoint"

### Specific UI/UX Suggestions

**Navigation:**
- Add "Get Started" wizard on first login
- Highlight "Most Used" features in sidebar
- Add breadcrumbs (Home > Audiences > Pet Supplies > Insights Report)

**Saved Cards:**
- Currently in sidebar—would benefit from dedicated "My Workspace" page
- Add folders/tags for organization ("Q1 Campaign", "Competitive Research", etc.)
- Show thumbnail previews instead of just list of titles

**Search:**
- Add search history ("Recent Searches")
- Suggest related searches ("People also searched for...")
- Show "Popular Searches" for inspiration

**Responsiveness:**
- Add keyboard shortcuts (Cmd+K for search, Cmd+S for save)
- Implement autosave for in-progress work
- Add "Recently Viewed" section
