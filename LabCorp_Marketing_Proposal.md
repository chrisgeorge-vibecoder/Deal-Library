## Executive Summary

Sovrn is pleased to present a comprehensive marketing strategy to help LabCorp reach health-conscious consumers across the United States. Through our advanced audience intelligence platform, we have identified and mapped precise audience segments that align with LabCorp's three core target personas:

1. **Health Seekers** – Consumers interested in comprehensive wellness, including men's and women's health
2. **Health Optimizers** – Individuals actively prioritizing optimal health through balanced diets and lifestyle habits
3. **Symptom Seekers** – People experiencing health issues seeking diagnosis, insight, and actionable health information

This proposal outlines our data-driven approach to reaching these audiences through **199 commerce audience segments** and **extensive interest-based targeting**, backed by real-time behavioral data and geographic intelligence.
