## ❓ **Anticipated Questions & Answers**

### **Q: How do you define these audiences?**
**A:** Behavioral data (content consumption, search behavior) + Purchase intent signals (active shopping) + Geographic data (ZIP code density). Refreshed every 7 days for accuracy.

### **Q: Can we target specific locations?**
**A:** Yes. ZIP code-level targeting lets us focus on areas near your testing centers and exclude low-performing regions.

### **Q: How is this different from Meta/Google?**
**A:** We provide access to premium health audiences across 10,000+ publisher sites. Not limited to social media or search. Reaches health-conscious consumers in-context while reading health content.

### **Q: What's the minimum spend?**
**A:** $50K for a 3-month pilot campaign. This allows us to test multiple audiences and optimize for performance.

### **Q: How do you track conversions?**
**A:** Conversion pixel tracks appointments, form fills, phone calls. UTM parameters track traffic source. Can integrate with your analytics platform.

### **Q: Is this HIPAA compliant?**
**A:** Yes. We use behavioral targeting only - no PII (personally identifiable information) or health records. Fully compliant with privacy regulations.
